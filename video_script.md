**Format:** Short-form, mixed Nepali/English, natural village setting
**Talent:** Aehi, on camera
**Platforms:** YouTube, TikTok/Reels, Facebook

---

*Aehi sitting outside in the village — natural light, trees/fields behind, maybe holding phone casually.*

*Quick cut: screen recording of HamroPatro — cluttered interface, ads everywhere. 2-3 seconds max.*

## PRODUCTION NOTES

### Setting
- Natural village environment — no studio, no ring light
- Morning or late afternoon light (golden hour if possible)
- Background: fields, trees, a courtyard, or a porch
- Ambient village sounds are fine (birds, wind) — adds authenticity

### Camera
- Phone camera is fine — use back camera for higher quality
- Someone else holds the camera, or use a simple tripod
- Mix of: sitting (hook), walking (features), standing still (closer)
- Screen recordings: do separately, overlay in edit

### Tone
- Casual, like explaining to a friend
- Not salesy — more "yo kura thaha chha?" (did you know this?)
- Speak at a natural pace, don't rush

### Editing
- Keep cuts tight — no dead air
- Screen recordings should be short (3-5 seconds each)
- Add subtle text overlays for key features (Nepali script)
- Background music: low, ambient, optional

### Screen Recordings Needed
1. HamroPatro app — cluttered home screen with ads (2-3 sec)
2. NagarikPatro calendar — clean daily view (3 sec)
3. Constitution view + Meaning Mode tap (5 sec)
4. Map → district tap → leader profile (5 sec)
5. Forex/gold prices screen (2 sec)
6. Photo merger tool (2 sec)
7. End card: logo + platform icons (3 sec)
